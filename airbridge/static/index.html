Delay test

          

        
Pings the NXR, places a call (with the configured DTMF), sends a 1 kHz tone burst and listens 10 s for it to come back.
          A true round trip needs something on the NXDN side to echo it, e.g. an NXDN radio set up as a parrot / echo on the group. Without one, you still get every part AirBridge adds.
          NXR audio is never transmitted on airband during the test.

        

      

      

        
Relay

        
Force the relay for wiring checks. Auto returns it to the configured mode.

        

      

      

        
Thresholds from live readings

        
With no signal, capture the floor. Then key a weak test carrier and capture it. RSSI and AF thresholds are both set between the two, with hysteresis.
